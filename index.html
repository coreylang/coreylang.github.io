Legal Aid Management System (LAMS)

The Legal Aid Management System (LAMS) is a PHP application built on the CodeIgniter framework to streamline daily operations for law firms. Hosted on Google Cloud Platform, it leverages MySQL and Redis for data storage and incorporates components developed in PHP, C#, JavaScript, Python, and Google Apps Script. Some of the application features include:

  • Contact and client data
  • Client case data
  • Manual and automatic billing
  • User/Employee time tracking
  • Document management and filing system
  • Legal document drafting and generation

  • Dynamic client portal and data entry
  • Work queues and attorney consultations
  • Business and efficiency reports
